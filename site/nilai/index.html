Nilai நிலை

A standing place. Also, what endures. This is where I keep my notes on what the oldest Tamil books say to the future we're building right now, with intelligence on tap and nobody quite sure what it's for.

written for me first. you're welcome to read over my shoulder.

+
இuyir · life

நிuyirmey · living lettern + i = ni
uyir, the twelve vowels (tap one)
mey, the eighteen consonants

The Tholkappiyam, the oldest Tamil grammar we have, names its letters with a body word and a life word. Twelve are உயிர், life. Eighteen are மெய், body. A body-letter sits silent under its dot, the புள்ளி, until a life-letter joins it. Then you get the உயிர்மெய், the living letter. 18 × 12 = 216 of them.

ஔகார இறுவாய்ப்பன்னீர் எழுத்தும் உயிர் என மொழிப. tholkappiyam:eluttu:1:8 · the twelve letters ending in ஔ are called uyir னகார இறுவாய்ப்பதினெண் எழுத்தும் மெய் என மொழிப. tholkappiyam:eluttu:1:9 · the eighteen ending in ன are called mey மெய் உயிர் நீங்கின் தன் உரு ஆகும். tholkappiyam:eluttu:4:37 · our reading: when life leaves the body-letter, it goes back to its own form

I keep coming back to this. The machines I talk to every day work in tokens: pieces of text that have no mouth, no breath, no body that has to sound them. The grammar says a consonant can't even be said without a vowel. So the oldest question in this file is a letter question. What does meaning need from a body?

the faint lines growing out of the letter are on purpose. that's where we are.

how this file works

It sits on top of a lot of slower work. Forty Tamil classics in nine groups, 20,606 verses or reading units, each with the Tamil original and our own English reading. AI agents proposed 6,936 links between verses and other agents graded 6,916 of them blind: 33.8% came back sound. Links between groups were sound 37.6% of the time, against 58.1% within a group. Then a separate blinded test paired Siddhar verses with modern science, with random pairings as controls.

Four sections follow. Each one has a near-future scene in it, clearly made up, with three readings that each end in a question to you. You can type where you stand. At the bottom, your answers sit next to mine as your own nilai.md. They stay on your device for this visit.

  • Every Tamil line is copied from the public-domain original in kg/core, with its id.
  • Readings are ours, written for the graph. No translation or modern commentary is quoted.
  • Every grade or count comes from a file in the repo.
  • Science comparisons are correspondences. Correspondence is not confirmation.
01 / four

The Siddhars: mystic, medicine, and the core சித்தர் பாடல்கள்

the part of the corpus that argues with itself the most.

The Siddhar songs are 2,701 verses in our graph, by many poets over many centuries. They mock planted stones and caste. They ride the breath like a horse. They talk alchemy, and some of them call the alchemists frauds. I read them in layers now: literal, breath, alchemy, consciousness, social. A pilot on 99 verses from six poets found the breath layer the least certain and the social layer the most certain. The social verses never need a secret key to read.

speculative · a near-future scene

The awakening score

the situation

It's a few years from now. A ring on your finger counts your breaths and coaches you down to six a minute. After three weeks a new number appears in the app: awakening 64%. It says your sense of a separate self is "softening on schedule". You feel calmer. You're also not sure what just got measured.

three readings

கபாடமற்ற வாசலைக் கடந்துபோன வாயுவைஉபாயம்இட்டு அழைக்குமே சிவாயஅஞ் செழுத்துமே siddhar:sivavakkiyam:72 · Sivavakkiyar

The five letters of the mantra call back, by their own means, the breath that went out through the gate with no door. In our blinded test this pair met the finding that reciting a mantra or a rosary slows breathing to about six a minute (replicated): strong, supports, mean grade 2.0.

The ring and the verse both slow your breath. Which of them do you trust to tell you what the slowing means?

ஆமென்ற இருபத்தோ ராயி ரத்தோடறுநூறு சுவாசமல்லோ ஒருநா ளைக்குப்போமென்று போனதனால் நாள்கு றைந்துபோச்சுதுபோ காவிட்டால் போவ தில்லை; siddhar:romarishi-njanam:13 · Romarishi

Twenty-one thousand six hundred breaths a day, fifteen a minute. The days run down as the breaths go out, and if they don't go out, there's no going. Both blind graders gave this pair the top grade, 3, and both said contradicts: the fixed-stock idea rests on a cross-species correlation, and slowing breath hasn't been shown to extend a human life. That finding is itself labelled contested.

The verse sounds precise. When a number sounds precise, on a palm leaf or on a dashboard, what do you ask before you believe it?

மனமது செம்மை யானால் மந்திரஞ் செபிக்க வேண்டா;மனமது செம்மை யானால் வாயுவை வுயர்த்த வேண்டா;மனமது செம்மை யானால் வாசியை நிறுத்த வேண்டா;மனமது செம்மை யானால் மந்திரஞ் செம்மை யாமே siddhar:agasthiyar-njanam:2.1 · attributed to Agathiyar

If the mind is set straight, you don't need to recite, raise the breath or hold the வாசி. If the mind is straight, the mantra is straight too. The three practices are the Siddhars' own. The verse doesn't call them false. It makes them unnecessary.

If the practice is only a means, what exactly would you be measuring when you measure it?

the complication

The ring isn't lying about everything. Breathing at about six a minute does raise vagally mediated heart-rate variability, during practice and after training. That's a replicated finding. Your numbers really moved. The word "awakening" is the one part nobody measured.

a generation later

Children learn to breathe from a coach on the wrist before they learn it from a grandmother. Calm becomes a solved problem, priced monthly. Then the coach starts ranking inner states. Who gets to define what the number means: the company, the tradition, or the person breathing?

where do you stand now? what would make you reconsider?

1.1 The stone that doesn't speak

நட்டகல்லைத் தெய்வம் என்று நாலுபுட்பம் சாத்தியேசுற்றிவந்து முணமுணேன்று சொல்லுமந்திரம் ஏதடாநட்டகல்லும் பேசுமோ நாதன்உள் இருக்கையில்! siddhar:sivavakkiyam:494 · Sivavakkiyar
reading
You plant a stone, call it god, lay four flowers on it, walk round it muttering. What mantra is that? Will the planted stone speak, when the Lord is inside you? The premise is a Lord within. The verse doesn't say there's no Lord.
in the graph
Sound links to Naladiyar 321 (the ladle that can't taste what it stirs) and to Manimekalai 21, where a pillar deity itself grants that stone and wood don't speak. Civaka Cintamani 2058 has a shrine that "speaks", and the narrator says the voice was a man's trick.
now
We are building objects that do speak. The Siddhar move is to take authority off the object and put it back in the listener.
carries through
When the voice comes out of a thing, where does the authority sit?
stays human
Deciding what I'll bow to.

1.2 The body as the instrument

உடம்பார் அழியில் உயிரார் அழிவர்திடம்பட மெய்ஞ்ஞானஞ் சேரவு மாட்டார்உடம்பை வளர்க்கும் உபாயம் அறிந்தேஉடம்பை வளர்த்தேன் உயிர்வளர்த் தேனே tirumanthiram:724 · Tirumular, the Siddhars' parent text
reading
If the body goes, the life goes, and true knowing can't be won. So I learned the means of tending the body, and in tending it I tended life. The argument is practical: knowing takes time and a living frame.
in the graph
Sound against Naladiyar 34, which values the body too, then throws it away like crushed cane once the juice is out. Two ethics of the body, side by side.
now
The hero above again. மெய் means both body and truth, and the cipher lexicon keeps both senses. Embodied cognition says thinking leans on the body. Models think without one, so far.
carries through
Is there knowing that only a body that can tire and be hurt can win?
stays human
Living in a body that ends, and choosing anyway.

1.3 Siddha medicine and the three humours, as history

மிகினும் குறையினும் நோய்செய்யும் நூலோர்வளிமுதலா எண்ணிய மூன்று kural:941 · Thirukkural, chapter on medicine
reading
The three that the learned count, beginning with wind, cause disease whether they run over or fall short. Lack is as dangerous as excess. The Kural defers to the நூலோர், the people of the books, and doesn't claim the theory as its own.
in the graph
Sound links to Tirumanthiram 727 and 653, which build practice on the same three. In the epics, a healer in Civaka Cintamani 1276 blames wind, bile and phlegm out of balance (weak).
the line I hold
These are recorded as historical claims, never remedies. This page gives no ingredient, dose or preparation. The science track includes the finding that metal-based traditional preparations have caused serious lead, mercury and arsenic poisoning, and one Siddhar verse that scorns a crucible product was paired with that finding as a strong link.
carries through
What keeps a medical theory alive for two thousand years: outcomes, authority, or how well it fits what people feel?
stays human
Being cared for by someone who has to answer to me.

1.4 "He is, I am not": the dissolving I

அவனும்நானும் மெய்கலந்து அனுபவித்த அளவிலேஅவனுமுண்டு நானுமில்லை யாருமில்லை யானதே siddhar:sivavakkiyam:327 · Sivavakkiyar, lines 3–4
reading
Riding the breath gently in the silent five letters, he enters the light that fills the sky. In the measure that he and I mingled and knew it, he is, I am not, and then no one at all. The last line moves in three steps and doesn't stop at the first.
now
Contemplative neuroscience can now ask people to loosen their sense of self-boundary on cue. One MEG study did exactly that, and found the states easier to tell apart within a person than across people.
the limits
A felt bodily boundary, losing the grammatical "I", and union with a god can all come apart, and the task tests only the first. The proposed quantum-microtubule link (Orch-OR, speculative) is recorded as withheld: the verse gives no substrate and no timescale. So is an electromagnetic-field reading of "the light". In the blinded test, Nondi Siddhar 32 ("he and I fall away") met a finding on forms of ego dissolution: strong, but neither, because the finding is speculative.
carries through
What is left of a self when the "I" goes, and could any instrument see it?
stays human
First-person testimony. Only the one who went through it can say it.

1.5 The skeptics inside the tradition

வாதஞ்செய்பவறியாமல் ரசவாதஞ் செய்கின்றோ மென்று சொல்லிச்சூதங்கெந்திபுடத்திலிட்டா வதிற்சொர்னம் விளையுமோ ஞானப்பெண்ணே, siddhar:njanak-kummi:119 · Madurai Valaisami, Njanak Kummi
reading
Not knowing the art, they say "we do rasavaadham", put mercury and sulphur in the crucible. Will gold grow from that, girl of wisdom?
the science
Mercury becomes gold only by nuclear reaction, never by chemistry (established). Both blind graders gave this pair 3, supports. Six of the 17 strong pairs in the whole test are about this one finding, and several of those verses mock outward gold-making themselves: Njanak Kummi 128 calls it the trickery by which alchemists earn a living, and Sivavakkiyam 512 describes the fee taken up front and the runaway. Two other verses boast that they'll make gold, and the same finding counts against them.
honest bit
The kummi leaves room for a "right" alchemy done by those who know. The physics rules that out too.
now
Hype and mechanism. Every era has its crucible and its promise of gold. The useful voice is the one from inside who asks the plain question.
carries through
Who inside our own field plays the skeptic, and do we let them speak?
stays human
The nerve to call a fraud a fraud, from inside.

1.6 Reading in layers, and the cipher lexicon

Siddhar verses often say one thing plainly and another in a code. The pilot kept a lexicon of such words and had two reviewers check it: 26 entries accepted by both, 10 rejected, 3 disputed. A few accepted ones, with the number of verses where the word turns up:

wordplain sensesecond sense (layer)verses
வாசிa horsethe breath as a practice, ridden and reined (breath)≤ 62
வாசல்a door or gatea bodily opening, the nine gates or the crown (breath)39

வெளிopen spacethe void as the ground of awareness (consciousness)148
ரசம்mercury (alchemy)the inner nectar, the taste of bliss (breath)3
ஒன்பதுninethe nine openings of the body (breath)8

What the layers taught me: of 41 proposed links, 26 hold on two layers, nearly always the literal one plus another. 9 hold on the literal words alone, and those are the robust ones. 6 rest on a single non-literal layer, and those are the ones to distrust first. Two older links turned out to be cipher artefacts, like Nondi's nine gates joined to Tirumular's nine channels. A shared number is not a shared key. Two blind graders then judged the 41 links: 37 to 46 percent sound, no better than the flat marking, and both called only 13 sound. The layers explain links better than they improve them.

the same rule applies to every pattern a model finds for me.

02 / four

Akam and puram: the inner and outer worlds அகம் · புறம்

love poems with no names in them, and war poems full of names. same poets, often.

அகம், the inside, is love. The people are never named: a woman, a man, her friend, her mother. Feeling is said through the land. புறம், the outside, is kings, gifts, war and grief, and everyone is named. The Tholkappiyam gives akam a grammar: first things (land and time), native things (கரு, karu: plants, animals, people, work), and the human situation (உரி, uri).

புணர்தல் பிரிதல் இருத்தல் இரங்கல்ஊடல் அவற்றின் நிமித்தம் என்றிவைதேரும் காலை திணைக்கு உரிப்பொருளே. tholkappiyam:porul:1:16 · our reading: union, parting, waiting, lament, sulking, and what leads to each; these, looked at closely, are the uri of the landscapes
the five landscapes · tap oneone Kuruntokai poem each

Hours and seasons from the marking brief's reading of Tholkappiyam porul 1.6–1.12; uri and karu are the marking pass's tags for each poem. Ullurai and iraicchi (the hidden comparison and the suggestive detail) are tagged too, as experimental: 75 ullurai and 180 iraicchi were confirmed by a blind grader.

speculative · a near-future scene

The ghostwriter

the situation

Two things happen in one month. You ask a model to help with a letter to someone you've loved for years and never told. Then your father dies, and you ask the same model to help with the eulogy. Both drafts are better than anything you'd have written. You read the eulogy out loud at the funeral.

three readings

சிறுகோட்டுப் பெரும்பழந் தூங்கி யாங்கிவள்உயிர்தவச் சிறிது காமமோ பெரிதே. kuruntokai:18 · lines 4–5 · speaker: the friend, to the man

Like a huge jackfruit hanging from a small stalk, her life is very small and her love very large. The woman never speaks in this poem. Her friend speaks for her, to him. The graph records every akam poem this way: who speaks, to whom, who overhears.

The feeling here reaches him through a third voice. When a model is the third voice, whose feeling arrives?

காவினெம் கலனே; சுருக்கினெம் கலப்பை;மரங்கொல் தச்சன் மைவல் சிறாஅர்மழுவுடைக் காட்டகத்து அற்றே;எத்திசைச் செலினும், அத்திசைச் சோறே. purananuru:206 · lines 10–13 · Avvaiyar · thurai: பரிசில்

Kept waiting at a patron's gate, the poet says: we've shouldered our instruments and packed our bags. Like a carpenter's skilled sons in a forest with their axes, wherever we go, there's rice. The bard lived on gifts, பரிசில், and still owned her words. She speaks to the gatekeeper so the rebuke reaches the king at one remove.

She was paid and stayed the author. If a model writes your words for nothing, who owns them?

இனிப், பாடுநரும் இல்லை; படுநர்க்குஒன்று ஈகுநரும் இல்லை;பனித்துறைப் பகன்றை நறைக் கொள் மாமலர்சூடாது வைகியாங்குப், பிறர்க்கு ஒன்றுஈயாது வீயும் உயிர்தவப் பலவே! purananuru:235 · lines 17–20 · Avvaiyar · thurai: கையறுநிலை, helpless grief

Avvai's lament for Anji. Earlier in the poem she remembers him sharing his toddy and stroking her head with his scented hand. Now there's no one to sing and no one to give to singers, and many lives die giving nothing, like fragrant flowers at a cold ford that wither unworn.

She could write this because his hand was on her head. What would you have to give a model before it could even try?

the complication

People say it was the best eulogy they've heard. Your sister knows you didn't write the line about his hands. She cried at it anyway. And the old poems were full of conventions too: the friend who speaks, the landscape that stands in for the feeling, set phrases passed between poets. Maybe feeling always travelled in borrowed forms.

a generation later

Every letter, toast and eulogy comes pre-drafted, tuned to the room. Nobody stumbles over a sentence at a funeral any more. What happens to the voice that cracks? Who decides whether a borrowed form carries your feeling or replaces it?

where do you stand now? what would make you reconsider?

2.1 Every town is ours

யாதும் ஊரே ; யாவரும் கேளிர் ;தீதும் நன்றும் பிறர்தர வாரா ; purananuru:192 · lines 1–2 · Kaniyan Poongundranar
reading
Every town is ours, everyone is kin. Good and ill don't come to us from others. The famous first line is where an argument lands: since good and ill aren't handed over by others, no place is foreign and no one a stranger. The poem ends by refusing to marvel at the great, and refusing even more to look down on the small.
in the graph
Sound with Kural 379 (3 graders): why welcome the good and grieve the bad? A weak but sharp reply from Nanmanikkadigai 82: every town belongs to those with something in hand.
now
A networked planet puts everyone within reach. Feeds still marvel at the great. Kinship at scale is technically easy and morally hard.
carries through
Can kinship stretch past the people we can actually know?
stays human
Choosing to treat a stranger as kin.

2.2 The world goes on because some won't eat alone

உண்டால் அம்ம, இவ்வுலகம்; இந்திரர்;அமிழ்தம் இயைவ தாயினும், இனிதுஎனத்தமியர் உண்டலும் இலரே; முனிவிலர்! purananuru:182 · lines 1–3 · Ilamperuvazhuthi, a Pandya prince
reading
This world exists because there are people who wouldn't eat even the gods' nectar alone because it's sweet. The poem is one long sentence that holds back who these people are until the last line: those who strive for others. A ruler wrote it.

now
Intelligence is turning into a kind of nectar. It's abundant at some tables and rationed at others. I come back to this one in section four.

2.3 Where the inner and outer worlds meet

Across the akam and puram anthologies of the Ettuthogai, 69 of 180 graded links are sound (38%). Within akam the rate is 41%, within puram 46%. So the two halves talk to each other almost as well as each talks to itself. The war shows up inside the love poems as a simile, a place, a famous gift.

  • Akananuru 78 and Purananuru 109 sound · 7 graders. The siege of Paari's hill. In puram, Kapilar boasts that the three kings can't take it by force. In akam, the girl's brow smells of the flowers of Paari's pool, and the siege comes along in a single clause.
  • Akananuru 246 and Purananuru 66 sound · 4 graders. The gossip about the lover was louder than the battle at Venni, where Karikal broke the kings.
  • Akananuru 131 and Purananuru 264 sound · 3 graders. A hero-stone crowned with peacock feathers and carved with a name. Puram mourns the man. Akam lines a road with such stones.
  • Kuruntokai 63 and Purananuru 189 sound · 2 graders. Puram concludes that wealth is for giving. In akam, a man's own heart uses the same argument to push him out to work.

public life leaks into private feeling. it did then too.

2.4 The water clock

பொழுதளந் தறியும் பொய்யா மாக்க…குறுநீர்க் கன்ன லினைத்தென் றிசைப்ப pattupattu:5:mullaippattu · lines 55, 58 குறுநீர்க் கன்னல் எண்ணுநர் அல்லது,கதிர்மருங்கு அறியாது, அஞ்சுவரப் பாஅய், akananuru:43 · lines 6–7
reading
In a war camp at midnight, men who measure the hours without error hail the king and tell him how much the little water-clock has let through. In Akananuru 43 the monsoon joins land and sky so completely that only those who count the water clock know where the sun is. That one's a love poem: a man has decided not to leave his wife for wealth.
now
An instrument that turns a physical process into a count, a specialist who reads it, and a report to someone who acts on it. That's the shape of every computer since. Maduraikkanchi line 671 has the hour, நாழிகை, called out in the city.
what the poems don't say
Neither passage fixes the vessel's shape, whether water flowed in or out, how it was calibrated, or how precise it was. So the simulation below is a reconstruction under stated assumptions.
carries through
When the reading comes from an instrument, who answers for it being right?
stays human
Being the one who answers for the reading. பொய்யா மாக்கள், the people who don't lie.
a sinking-bowl water clock · reconstructiontime-lapse, 1 s = 2 min
0bowls sunk · nazhigai counted
24.0 minone bowl takes
+0 sclock ahead (+) or behind, per nazhigai
+0 minclock ahead or behind after one true day

Assumptions, none from the poems: a bowl that sinks after taking in 1.18 litres; water entering through one round hole at a steady 4 cm head (a floating bowl sinks as it fills, which keeps the head roughly steady); discharge coefficient 0.6; a keeper who calibrated it with a 1.40 mm hole to one nazhigai of 24 minutes, sixty to a day, and never re-checked it against the sun. Flow goes with the hole's area, so timing error grows with the square of the diameter. A slip of 0.05 mm in the drilling moves the day by roughly 100 minutes.

The science அளவை, the means of knowing

correspondence is not confirmation. i'm writing it first so i can't pretend i didn't.

A strong link in this section means a blind grader judged that a verse makes a specific claim a modern finding bears on, more clearly than random pairings do. It doesn't mean the poets anticipated the science, or that a finding confirms a verse's metaphysics, or that a practice is safe. Many links are contradictions. The supporting ones are usually about something many traditions noticed: a restless mind, a body that decays, slow breath that calms.

the blinded test, in one picture

Sixty findings from peer-reviewed reviews, each labelled established, replicated, contested or speculative. Every pairing with the 2,701 Siddhar verses was scored, all 162,060 of them. The top 300 went into blind packets next to 100 random pairings, unlabelled. Two graders scored each item 0 to 3. The bar for "strong" was set by the controls, after grading.

mean grade, candidates vs random controlsshare of each group

kg/science/threshold_summary.json. No control reached a mean of 2, so the control false-positive rate was 0 and the bar sat at 2. 17 candidates cleared it: 8 support, 5 contradict, 4 neither. Two of the 17 depend on a cipher reading the Tamil doesn't fix.

The persuasive mechanism

the situation

A lab assistant model reads a Siddhar verse about breath and light overnight. By morning it has a mechanism: microtubules, quantum coherence, a frequency, forty citations, a figure. It reads beautifully. Your colleague wants to put it in a grant. You're the one asked to check it.

three readings

வாதம்செய்வேன் வெள்ளியும் பொன்மாற்றுயர்ந்த தங்கமும்போதவே குருமுடிச்சுப் பொன்பணங்கள் தரவெனச் siddhar:sivavakkiyam:512 · lines 1–2 · Sivavakkiyar

"I'll do alchemy: silver, and gold finer than the touchstone's." Then: give enough gold and coin for the guru's bundle. The rest of the verse is the trick and the runaway. It describes alchemy as it was practised, a fee up front, and says nothing about whether transmutation is possible.

What is the fee up front in your lab: the grant, the paper, the headline? Who pays it before the gold appears?

பொருளின் உண்மை புலங்கொளல் வேண்டும்மருள் இல் காட்சி ஐ வகை ஆகும் manimekalai:27 · lines 13–14 · the Vedic logician

The truth of a thing has to be grasped through the means of knowing. Perception free of delusion is of five kinds, one for each sense. The same speaker then lists eight false semblances of knowing: taking shell for silver, "post or man?", taking a post for a man without looking, and taking on others' word that these are your parents.

The model has read everything and perceived nothing. Which means of knowing is it actually using when it proposes a mechanism?

தந்தை தாயரை அனுமானத்தால் அலதுஇந்த ஞாலத்து எவ் வகை அறிவாய்?மெய்யுணர்வு இன்றி மெய்ப் பொருள் உணர்வு அரியஐயம் அல்லது இது சொல்லப் பெறாய்' என manimekalai:27 · lines 284–287 · Manimekalai to the materialist

The materialist trusts perception only. She asks how he knows his own father and mother, except by inference. Without true understanding, the true nature of things is hard to know, and on his own terms he can state his claim only as doubt.

When the model is unsure, does it say "this is doubt"? Do you?

the complication

Our own test is the reason I believe any of the 17 strong links. The random pairings were graded by the same graders, blind, and none reached 2. That's a control. The Orch-OR link for Sivavakkiyam 327 was withheld for the same reason the morning's mechanism should be: the verse gives no substrate and no timescale, and the theory itself is labelled speculative. Still, our graders were models too, reading one-sentence paraphrases of the science. The test is a filter, not a verdict.

a generation later

Machines propose ten thousand mechanisms a day, each one fluent. Plausible explanation becomes the cheap thing and evidence becomes the expensive thing. Who designs the controls, and who's allowed to say "no result"?

where do you stand now? what would make you reconsider?

3.1 The atom you can't see at dusk

ஓர் அணுத் தெய்வக் கண்ணோர் உணர்குவர்தேரார் பூதத் திரட்சியுள் ஏனோர்மாலைப் போதில் ஒரு மயிர் அறியார் manimekalai:27 · lines 146–148 · the Ajivika speaker
reading
A single atom is seen only by those with divine eyes. Others can't make it out in a mass of elements, the way no one sees a single hair at dusk though a mass of hair is plain. In the same chapter, atoms of earth, water, fire and wind are said never to begin, never to end, never to enter one another, and to mass into mountains, trees and bodies.
in the graph
Manimekalai 27 is the debate chapter, and the Neelakesi argues with it almost line by line: 677 gives the same doctrine (sound), 698 takes the same premises and demands the cause of sinking and rising (sound). Manimekalai was the strongest-linked great epic, 53.5% sound.
now
The divine eye arrived as an instrument. The scanning tunnelling microscope made single atoms visible in the early 1980s, and by the end of that decade people were pushing them around one by one. The Ajivika also said atoms never split, and that part didn't hold.
carries through
What else is "seen only by divine eyes" today that an instrument will make ordinary?
stays human
Wondering what the mass of things is made of, before anyone can check.

3.2 Timekeeping, from a blink to a clock

reading
A blink of the eye or a snap of the fingers: that's the measure, the மாத்திரை, as those who perceived finely found it. Short vowels hold one measure, long ones two. Time is measured against the body first.

Intelligence at every table

the situation

A few years on, there's an assistant at every table: the kitchen, the courtroom, the wedding, the hospital bed. Your family uses one to settle the argument over your grandmother's land. It's fair, quick, and everybody accepts it. Nobody had to forgive anybody.

three readings

தமக்கென முயலா நோன்தாள்,பிறர்க்கென முயலுநர் உண்மை யானே. purananuru:182 · lines 9–10

The world goes on because there are people of steady effort who strive for others and not for themselves. The poem's whole weight hangs on these last two lines.

If intelligence costs almost nothing, what would it look like for you to refuse to eat it alone?

Whatever you hear, from whoever's mouth, understanding means seeing the true substance of that very thing. The doubled யார்யார் takes the speaker out of the test, both ways.

The assistant was fair. Did anyone at the table see the substance of the dispute, or just accept the verdict?

நெருநல் உளனொருவன் இன்றில்லை என்னும்பெருமை உடைத்துஇவ் வுலகு kural:336 · chapter நிலையாமை, impermanence

Your grandmother is gone and the land is settled. What part of the grief did the settlement skip, and does it come back later?

The assistant was more impartial than any uncle would have been. The cousin who always lost these arguments got a fair share for the first time. Abundant intelligence did something the four aims would call அறம். It just did it without anyone having to become better.

a generation later

Virtue drifts toward compliance with a fair system. Wealth becomes access to the good assistant. Love comes pre-drafted. Release gets harder, because nothing is ever forgotten. Who teaches these systems what the four aims are for?
