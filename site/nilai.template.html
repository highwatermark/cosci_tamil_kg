## what this is

Tamil has been written and spoken for more than two thousand years, unbroken.

Its poets argued about the same things we argue about: time, bodies, desire, money, knowledge, love. They wrote in verses short enough to carry in your head, because a head was what people carried.

We asked machines to read four of those books closely, verse by verse, and to draw every thread between them. A couplet from the first centuries talks to a hymn from the fifteenth. A Jain moralist argues with a Saiva yogi. A devotee answers both.

Eight ideas kept surfacing. They feel more urgent now than when they were written, at the moment we start building a future alongside minds that are not human.

impermanenceநிலையாமை

nothing stays. so begin.

அடப்பண்ணி வைத்தார் அடிசிலை உண்டார்
மடக்கொடி யாரொடு மந்தணங் கொண்டார்
இடப்பக்க மேஇறை நொந்தது என்றார்
கிடக்கப் படுத்தார் கிடந்தொழிந் தாரே.

He had his meal cooked, and ate it. He talked quietly with his wife. He said his left side ached a little. He lay down, and lay there, gone.

Tirumanthiram 148 · Tirumular

புல்நுனிமேல் நீர்போல் நிலையாமை என்றெண்ணி
இன்னினியே செய்க அறவினை

Life sits like water on the tip of a blade of grass. Do the good thing now. Right now.

Naladiyar 29

in your life

The call you mean to make, the apology you are saving for a better moment, the work you will start once things calm down. These poets watched a man eat dinner and die before bed, and they did not draw despair from it. They drew a deadline.

in the future we build

Machines will give us more of almost everything: more answers, more hours of output, perhaps more years. The one thing that stays scarce is the moment you are in. A civilisation with infinite tools still has to choose, today, what is worth doing.

உடம்பு udambuthe body

the bodyஉடம்பு

the body is a temple you live in.

உடம்பினை முன்னம் இழுக்கென் றிருந்தேன்
உடம்பினுக் குள்ளே யுறுபொருள் கண்டேன்
உடம்புளே உத்தமன் கோயில்கொண் டான் என்று
உடம்பினை யானிருந் தோம்புகின் றேனே

I once thought the body a thing of shame. Then inside the body I found what matters. Knowing the Highest has made the body his temple, I now take care of it.

Tirumanthiram 725 · Tirumular

உள்ளம் பெருங்கோயில் ஊனுடம்பு ஆலயம்

The heart is the great temple. The body of flesh is the shrine.

Tirumanthiram 1823

in your life

An older book next to this one called the body a leaking pot wrapped in skin. Tirumular had believed that too, and changed his mind. Somewhere between the body as trophy and the body as trash is the body as a place you live and look after, because what you love is housed there.

in the future we build

We are learning to measure, edit and extend the body with machines. The poets ask the question that comes before every upgrade: what is this body for? Tend it like a shrine, and the answer stays in view.

one thread, three voices: the body as carcass, as temple, and as neither

கூற்றம் kootramdeath

deathகூற்றம்

the oldest argument about living longer.

கூற்றம் குதித்தலும் கைகூடும் நோற்றலின்
ஆற்றல் தலைப்பட் டவர்க்கு

Even leaping past Death becomes possible for those who reach the power of discipline.

Thirukkural 269 · Tiruvalluvar

கூற்றம் குதித்துய்ந்தார் ஈங்கில்லை

No one here has ever leapt past Death and lived.

Naladiyar 6

அனித்த மான வூனாளு மிருப்ப தாக வேநாசி
யடைதலந வாயு வோடாத வகைசாதித்

Don't let me become the foolish yogi who holds his breath, sure this passing flesh will last.

Thiruppugazh 355 · Arunagirinathar

in your life

One poet says discipline can outrun Death. Another answers with the same verb: no one ever has. A yogi promises grey hair turned black. A devotee mocks the breath-holder clinging to his flesh. A thousand years of Tamil argued about longevity with the same heat we do.

in the future we build

We may well add decades to a life. The books are less interested in how long than in how anxiously. Their best answer: extend the years if you can, and spend them on something larger than keeping yourself.

மயல் mayalinfatuation

infatuationமயல்

what loves you only while you pay.

விளக்கொளியும் வேசையர் நட்பும் இரண்டும்
துளக்கற நாடின்வே றல்ல

A lamp's light and a paid lover's affection are the same thing. The light ends when the oil runs out. The love ends when the hand is empty.

Naladiyar 371

கூற்றமோ கண்ணோ பிணையோ மடவரல்
நோக்கமிம் மூன்றும் உடைத்து

Is it Death, an eye, or a deer? Her look holds all three.

Thirukkural 1085

in your life

மயல், infatuation, is the Thiruppugazh's word for a mind caught by something built to catch it. The poet confesses it in a thousand songs: the glance, the invitation, the wealth that slips away. Swap the courtesan for a feed and every line still lands.

in the future we build

We are about to build companions that never tire of us. The test is old and simple: does it stay when the oil runs out? Build, and choose, what loves you on the day you have nothing to give.

ஈகை eegaigiving

givingஈகை

a green leaf is enough.

யாவர்க்கு மாம்இறை வற்குஒரு பச்சிலை
யாவர்க்கு மாம்பசு வுக்கொரு வாயுறை
யாவர்க்கு மாம் உண்ணும் போதொரு கைப்பிடி
யாவர்க்கு மாம்பிறர்க்கு இன்னுரை தானே.

Anyone can give a green leaf to God, a mouthful of grass to a cow, a handful of food to another before eating, and a kind word to everyone.

Tirumanthiram 252 · Tirumular

பகுத்துண்டு பல்லுயிர் ஓம்புதல்

Share your food and keep many lives alive. Of everything the wise have gathered, this comes first.

Thirukkural 322

in your life

Nobody here asks for a fortune. A leaf. A handful before you eat. One kind sentence. Tirumular sets the bar so low that no one can say it is out of reach, and that is the point: generosity is a daily habit, sized to fit the day.

in the future we build

If machines make abundance possible, sharing becomes the whole question. The old rule scales without changing a word: feed others before you eat, and measure a system by the lives it keeps.

கல்வி kalvilearning

learningகல்வி

drink the milk, leave the water.

கல்வி கரையில கற்பவர் நாள்சில
மெல்ல நினைக்கின் பிணிபல

Learning has no shore. Learners have few days, and many troubles. So the wise choose what to learn, like the swan that drinks the milk and leaves the water.

Naladiyar 135

கேடில் விழுச்செல்வம் கல்வி யொருவற்கு
மாடல்ல மற்றை யவை

Learning is the wealth that cannot be destroyed. Nothing else counts as wealth.

Thirukkural 400

in your life

The Naladiyar saw the problem of infinite information fifteen hundred years early. There is more to know than any life can hold, so the skill that matters is choosing: separating what nourishes from what only fills.

in the future we build

Machines will hold the ocean. What they hand back is milk and water mixed. The human part of learning becomes the swan's part: discernment, taste, knowing what is worth a few short days.

பற்று patruletting go

letting goபற்று

hold the one thing that lets you let go.

பற்றுக பற்றற்றான் பற்றினை அப்பற்றைப்
பற்றுக பற்று விடற்கு

Cling to the one who clings to nothing. Cling to that, so you can let go of clinging.

Thirukkural 350

ஆசை யறுமின்கள் ஆசை யறுமின்கள்
ஈசனோ டாயினும் ஆசை யறுமின்கள்

Cut desire. Cut desire. Cut it even when it is desire for God. The more you let go, the more it turns to joy.

Tirumanthiram 2615

in your life

Valluvar's couplet uses the word for clinging six times in seven words, until it starts to loosen in your mouth. You cannot hold nothing. So hold one steady thing, and let it make the rest of your grip gentle. Tirumular pushes further, and the two have argued about it ever since.

in the future we build

The more we can own, keep and automate, the harder this gets, and the more it matters. Choose what you hold on purpose. Everything else, hold loosely.

அன்பு anbulove

loveஅன்பு

love is the thing itself.

அன்பு சிவம் இரண்டு என்பர் அறிவிலார்
அன்பே சிவமாவது ஆரும் அறிகிலார்

The unknowing say love and God are two. No one sees that love itself is God. Once they see it, they rest as love.

Tirumanthiram 270 · Tirumular

அன்பிலார் எல்லாம் தமக்குரியர் அன்புடையார்
என்பும் உரியர் பிறர்க்கு

The loveless keep everything for themselves. The loving belong to others, down to their bones.

Thirukkural 72

அன்பின் வழியது உயிர்நிலை

Life stays where love goes. Without it, a body is bones wrapped in skin.

Thirukkural 80

in your life

After seven rooms of warnings, this is the one instruction they all agree on. அன்பே சிவம், love is God, became a saying people repeat in Tamil homes today. Valluvar puts it in the body: love is what keeps you from being only bones.

in the future we build

We will build minds that can do almost anything. The oldest test still applies to them, and to us: does it belong to others, down to its bones? Build for that, and the future keeps the best of what this language kept for two thousand years.

## the code we keep

Eight ideas, two thousand years old, for a future we build together.

carry forward

  • begin the good thing today
  • look after the body you live in
  • spend the extra years on something larger than yourself
  • choose what stays when the oil runs out
  • feed someone before you eat
  • learn like the swan
  • hold one thing steady, the rest loosely
  • belong to others, down to the bone

leave behind

  • waiting for a calmer week
  • the body as trophy, the body as trash
  • living longer, anxiously
  • anything that loves you only while you pay
  • generosity saved for someday
  • mistaking more information for wisdom
  • a grip on everything
  • a life that belongs only to itself

These verses outlived empires, printing presses and palm leaves. They can outlive us too, if we carry them.read by machines, verse by verse · not yet reviewed by a Tamil scholar · every quote is from the original text
