# Corpus groups and how they connect

The corpus is read in groups. Each group has its own core concerns, and links are graded within a group
and between groups separately. That way we can see whether a group's internal links are stronger
than the bridges between groups (so far they are not: see docs/reading-order.md and the grading
summaries).

| Group | Books | Core concern | Frame for marking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ettuthogai, akam | Natrinai, Kuruntokai, Ainkurunuru, Kalittokai, Akananuru | inner life of love, read through landscape | Tholkappiyam: thinai, uri, karu with role, speaker and addressee |
| Ettuthogai, puram | Purananuru, Patitruppattu | outer life: kings, gifts, war, death, fame | puram thinai and thurai, entities, meyppadu |
| Ettuthogai, devotional | Paripadal | early devotion to Murugan and Thirumal | sangam-marking-brief, with links to the Thiruppugazh |
| Pattupattu | the ten idylls | guide poems, cities, the god Murugan | reading units with puram/akam fields |
| Pathinenkilkanakku, ethics | Kural, Naladiyar, Nanmanikkadigai, Innaa and Iniyavai Narpathu, Thirikadugam, Acharakkovai, Pazhamozhi, Sirupanchamoolam, Muthumozhikkanchi, Elathi | conduct, counting and listing ethics | the Kural/Naladiyar marking, plus a numbered-list structure |
| Pathinenkilkanakku, akam | Kar Narpathu, the Ainthinai and Thinai works, Kainnilai, Kalavazhi (puram: battle) | akam in short form | as for Ettuthogai akam |
| Epics, great | Silappathikaram, Manimekalai, Cintamani, Valayapathi, Kundalakesi | justice, karma and renunciation in story; Jain and Buddhist ethics | narrative units: who acts, what is claimed, what is taught |
| Epics, minor | Soolamani, Neelakesi, Naga Kumara, Udayana Kumara, Yasodhara | Jain doctrine and debate | as for the great epics, plus doctrine claims |
| Devotional and mystic | Tirumanthiram, Thiruppugazh, Siddhar songs | inner divinity, yoga, devotion, critique of ritual | siddhar-layers lexicon and the stance field |
| Grammar | Tholkappiyam | the theory of meaning itself | used as the frame; its nurpas are cited, not linked |

**Bridges between groups that we test.** Each candidate bridge is graded blind, and a bridge
counts only if it beats the within-group baseline:
- akam landscape to mystic inner landscape
- puram ethics of giving to the ethics works
- epics' karma and renunciation to the Siddhars and Naladiyar
- Murugan across Paripadal, Thirumurugatruppadai and the Thiruppugazh

Sources: the original Tamil only, from Project Madurai, cross-checked against the sangamtamil.com text
and arbitrated by tamilvu. No modern translation or commentary is stored.
