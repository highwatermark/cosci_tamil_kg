## LINK pattupattu:1:227-249 -> tirumanthiram:197 type=TENSION
Note: The idyll's rite offers rice mixed with a ram's blood and spreads millet with blood, and calls it worship that brings each seeker what he sought; Tirumular says that in worship the bright flower is not killing other small lives. Read plainly, one counts a killing as part of right worship and the other counts not killing as the best offering.
Teaches: Blood offering stands in the oldest Murugan poem as an ordinary part of effective worship, so Tirumular's rule that not killing is the true flower is a reform of an existing Tamil practice, not a restatement of it.

Note: Paripadal thirattu 12 says those who live in Koodal and Parankunram are the ones truly living, and asks who would go to the world of the gods; Paripadal 15.15-18 keeps heaven as the goal and praises Maalirunkunram as the easy way to it.
