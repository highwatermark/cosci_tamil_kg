## LINK pazhamozhi_nanooru:14 -> kural:720 type=DEVELOPS

## LINK pazhamozhi_nanooru:11 -> naladiyar:270 type=TENSION
Note: Naladiyar 270 says those with understanding are as the learned though they have not studied; Pazhamozhi 11 says one who has neither studied nor listened does not understand clearly and is right only by chance. The conflict holds on the reading that 270's உணர்வுடையார் need no study or hearing at all.
Teaches: The pair splits on whether native understanding can stand in for learning: the Naladiyar lets inner sense make a man learned, Pazhamozhi treats an untaught man's right judgement as a windfall.
List adds: its proverb turns the untaught man's success into chance, the very case the quatrain credits to inner understanding.
