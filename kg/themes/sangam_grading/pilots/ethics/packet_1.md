## LINK acharakkovai:10 -> siddhar:1177 type=TENSION
Note: Acharakkovai 10 makes bathing the removal of impurity on ten occasions; Sivavakkiyar addresses those who bathe daily and whose dirt never goes, asking where the dirt really lies.
Teaches: The Siddhar denies the premise the bathing list rests on, that the impurity is where water can reach, whereas the Kural only ranks it below inner purity.

### acharakkovai:10 (Acharakkovai 10)
   1 தேவர் வழிபாடு தீக்கனா வாலாமை
   2 உண்டது கான்றல் மயிர்களைதல் ஊண்பொழுது
   3 வைகு துயிலொடு இணைவிழைச்சுக் கீழ்மக்கள்
   4 மெய்யுறல் ஏனை மயலுறல் ஈரைந்தும்
   5 ஐயுறாது ஆடுக நீர்
Marked reading: The verse lists ten occasions on which, it says, one bathes without hesitation: worship of the gods, a bad dream, uncleanness, vomiting what one has eaten, cutting the hair, mealtime, rising from sleep, sexual union, bodily contact with low people, and the other, falling into confusion.
Marker's nuance: Ten is reached by taking ஊண்பொழுது and வைகு துயில் as two occasions; ஏனை மயலுறல் is obscure and may mean any other bewilderment. கீழ்மக்கள் can be a moral term (the base), as often in the Kural, but a contact that calls for a bath points to a birth-group, so it is read as a caste term here. வாலாமை, uncleanness, may include menstrual or death pollution; the verse does not say whose.

### siddhar:1177: Siddhar 1177 (சிவவாக்கியர், சிவவாக்கியம்)
Tamil: அழுக்கறத் தினங்குளித்து அழுக்கறாத மாந்தரே, / அழுக்கிருந்தது எவ்விடம், அழகில்லாதது எவ்விடம். / அழுக்கிருந்தது எவ்விடத்து அழுக்கறுக்க வல்லீரேல் / அழுக்கிலாதது சோதியோடு அணுகிவாழ லாகுமே
Marked reading: You who bathe daily to remove the dirt and whose dirt is never removed: where was the dirt, and where is what is without beauty? If you can cut the dirt in the place where the dirt was, you can live close to the Light that has no dirt.
Marker's nuance: அழுக்கு is said seven times. The question 'where was the dirt' is the whole argument: if the dirt is in the mind, water cannot reach it. அழகில்லாதது (what has no beauty) is odd beside அழுக்கு and may be a play on the sound; the Light without dirt is the Lord.

Teaches: The akam couplet praises mutual desire from inside love; the ethics verse states the reverse as a rule of conduct, so the need for mutual desire is affirmed in both kinds of poetry.
Item: 2 (item 2 of thirikadugam:5's marked items)

Teaches: Pazhamozhi moves the Kural's rule of assembly into the field of ozhukkam, so speaking to the wrong audience is a fault of character that lowers the speaker, not only a tactical error.
List adds: it ranks the act as the worst of things for a man and proves it with the conduct maxim, which the couplet does not invoke.
