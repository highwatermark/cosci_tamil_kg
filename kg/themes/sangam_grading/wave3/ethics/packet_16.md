## LINK muthumozhikkanchi:49 -> kural:261 type=DEVELOPS

Teaches: The Kural defines penance by its content, endurance and harmlessness, while the saying defines it by how it is undertaken, so the pair shows two separate tests a vow must pass.
List adds: It sets penance as the twin of giving in a matched pair of lines, putting the ascetic's vow under the same test of right manner as the householder's gift, which the couplet does not.

Marker's fields: domain: ascetic

### kural:261: Kural 261
Tamil: உற்றநோய் நோன்றல் உயிர்க்குறுகண் செய்யாமை / அற்றே தவத்திற் குரு
Pope: The nature of religious discipline consists, in the endurance (by the ascetic) of the sufferings which it brings on himself, and in abstaining from giving pain to others.

### naladiyar:56: Naladiyar 56
Tamil: மாண்ட குணத்தொடு மக்கட்பே றில்லெனினும் / பூண்டான் கழித்தற் கருமையால் - பூண்ட / மிடியென்னும் காரணத்தின் மேன்முறைக் கண்ணே / கடியென்றார் கற்றறிந் தார்.
Pope: Though your wife possess no excellence and bear no child, it is hard to get rid of the marriage bond. For this cause since he who weds puts sorrow on, in olden days the learned made marriage a synonym of dread.
Marked reading: Even if a wife lacks good qualities and bears no children, the husband cannot put her away; because marriage brings this hardship, the learned gave it the name கடி.
Marker's nuance: The argument turns on a pun, since கடி means both marriage and 'shun'. The verse finds no fault in marriage to a good wife; it argues from the worst case that the bond, once made, can't be undone.
