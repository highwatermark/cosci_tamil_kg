Teaches: The couplet needs an argument to make the buyer guilty, while the verse simply lists buying and killing as two faults, showing the group's code taking the Kural's conclusion as settled.
Item: 2 (item 2 of nanmanikkadigai:26's marked items)

Teaches: Harmless idle talk passes the one test and fails the other, so the two chapters set different standards for speech.

### kural:200: Kural 200
Tamil: சொல்லுக சொல்லிற் பயனுடைய சொல்லற்க / சொல்லிற் பயனிலாச் சொல்
Pope: Speak what is useful, and speak not useless words.
Marked reading: Say what among words has use; do not say what among words has none.
Marker's nuance: Parimelazhagar notes that சொல்லில் is grammatically surplus and kept for the figure of repeated words. It may be a quiet wit that a chapter against waste in speech ends on a deliberate surplus, though that is my reading and no commentator's.

### kural:291: Kural 291
Tamil: வாய்மை எனப்படுவது யாதெனின் யாதொன்றும் / தீமை இலாத சொலல்
Pope: Truth is the speaking of such words as are free from the least degree of evil (to others).
Marked reading: If you ask what truth is, it is speech containing no evil at all.
Marker's nuance: A surprising definition, because nothing here refers to facts. Parimelazhagar spells out the consequence: stating what happened isn't the definition, and even an accurate report is falsehood if it harms.

## VERSE iniyavai_narpathu:26 (Iniyavai Narpathu 26)

## VERSE thirikadugam:63 (Thirikadugam 63)

## VERSE thirikadugam:35 (Thirikadugam 35)

## VERSE thirikadugam:42 (Thirikadugam 42)
