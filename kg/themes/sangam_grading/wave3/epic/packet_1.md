## LINK kundalakesi:2 -> kundalakesi:17 type=TENSION
Note: Verse 2 says the slips of speech of one praising the faultless are no slips; verse 17 says a fault stays a fault even in those who have reached truth and in a god.
Teaches: The fragments hold two standards side by side: the poet excuses faults by the worth of what is praised, while the moral verse refuses to let any height excuse a fault, so the apology rests on devotion, not on a rule about faults.
