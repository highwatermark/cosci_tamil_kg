## LINK civaka_cintamani:188 -> civaka_cintamani:208 type=TENSION
Note: 188 likens the wedded pair to the three-eyed god and his goddess joined; in 208 a counsellor cites the god's taking the mountain king's daughter into half his body as a great blame (பெரும் பழி).
Teaches: The same divine union serves the narrator as the highest praise of the couple and the counsellor as a warning of shame from desire, so the epic lets its own simile be turned against the king within twenty verses.
