## LINK civaka_cintamani:343 -> naladiyar:51 type=DEVELOPS
Note: Naladiyar 51 uses lamp and darkness to state that sin cannot stand before austerity and returns when good deeds are spent; 343 makes the departure of darkness at sunrise a figure for evil deeds' suffering leaving when old merit comes forward, on the road to a hermitage.
Teaches: The quatrain's doctrine becomes the epic's narrative weather: the dawn that ends Vijayai's flight is the merit-and-darkness image itself, placed just before she enters the very austerity the quatrain says sin cannot face.
