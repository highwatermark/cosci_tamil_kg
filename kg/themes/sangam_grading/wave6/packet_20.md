## LINK soolamani:140 -> kural:549 type=TENSION
Note: Kural 549 counts guarding the people and removing wrongdoing as the king's work and no fault; verse 140 faults a king on the ground of grace toward the lowest lives, which the Kural's list does not ask of him.
Teaches: The epic widens the king's duty to grace toward all lowly lives, where the Kural's duty stops at the people and the removal of wrongdoers, so the two hold different measures of a good ruler.
Gains: epic side: A rival standard, the Kural's, by which the king is judged only on what he does to his people. | other side: A test of its limit: a king who punishes wrongdoers by the Kural's rule may still lack grace toward lower creatures.
