## LINK civaka_cintamani:553 -> kural:1103 type=DEVELOPS
Note: The Kural's lover asks whether the lotus-eyed god's world is sweeter than sleep on a beloved's arms; the Vidyadhara king says even the endless pleasures of the Jain land of enjoyment are misery alone, and sleep in a lover's lap is the gods' own state.
