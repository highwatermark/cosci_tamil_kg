Note: Kural 376 says what is not one's own will not stay though guarded, nor what is one's own leave though thrown away; Pazhamozhi 231 shows a palmyra guarded all night whose fruit still falls to the one it is due to.
Teaches: The pairing turns the couplet's abstract 'guard' into a watchman shouting through the night under a tree, and adds a second person the couplet lacks, the passer-by who gets the fruit, so the loss of the guarder is someone else's unearned gain.
