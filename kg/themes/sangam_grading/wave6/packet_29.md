### naladiyar:34: Naladiyar 34
Tamil: அரும்பெறல் யாக்கையைப் பெற்ற பயத்தால் / பெரும்பயனும் ஆற்றவே கொள்க - கரும்பூர்ந்த / சாறுபோல் சாலவும் பின்உதவி மற்றதன் / கோதுபோல் போகும் உடம்பு.
Pope: As the gain from the mortal frame now reached —and which is so hard to reach —with all thy might lay hold of virtue's lasting good. As the juice expressed from the sugar-cane 'twill afterwards be thine aid, when the body goes like refuse flung away.
Marked reading: With the gain of having obtained this rare body, take its great fruit fully; like juice pressed from cane, it serves you afterwards, and the body goes like the crushed refuse.
Marker's nuance: The body is valued only as the cane is: for what can be pressed out of it. The juice, virtue, outlasts the body and is what helps 'afterwards'.

### naladiyar:81: Naladiyar 81
