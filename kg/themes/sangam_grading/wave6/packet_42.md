Teaches: One text holds that the senses are conquered by the ascetic's own labour, the other that they are conquered by meeting the Lord, so the two disagree on who does the work.

Marked reading: The women like golden creepers lament: is he the tall Vel of the long kadamba, or the son of the five arrows and the long bow? We cannot tell; tell us.

### kural:1081: Kural 1081
Tamil: அணங்குகொல் ஆய்மயில் கொல்லோ கனங்குழை / மாதர்கொல் மாலும் என் நெஞ்சு
Pope: Is this jewelled female a celestial, a choice peahen, or a human being ? My mind is perplexed.
Marked reading: The man, on first seeing her, cannot tell whether this woman in heavy earrings is a goddess, a rare peahen or a human being, and his heart reels.
Marker's nuance: The three guesses aren't mere compliments. Parimelazhagar ties each to evidence: her indescribable form and his distress suggest a goddess, her grace and the grove suggest a peahen, and the way she looks back suggests a woman. Kalaignar's rendering drops the goddess and gives 'beauty that torments me' instead.
