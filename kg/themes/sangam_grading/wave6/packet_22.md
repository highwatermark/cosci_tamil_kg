Note: Kural 1051 says that if the worthy giver withholds, the blame is his and not the asker's; Pazhamozhi 384 shows a never-refusing giver hiding what he has because many ask one thing, and puts the fault on the crowd of askers.
Teaches: The Kural places the blame for withholding on the giver; Pazhamozhi names a case where withholding comes from pity for those who would be turned away, so the blame moves to the number of askers.
