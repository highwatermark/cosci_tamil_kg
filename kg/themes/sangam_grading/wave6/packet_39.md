## LINK akananuru:43 -> akananuru:53 type=TENSION
Note: In 43 the man calls leaving a companion for wealth folly (மடமை) and pities those who do it; in 53 the heroine says her man left because his heart could not refuse the needy, a virtue she still reads as love of wealth over her.
Teaches: Read together they show the paalai debate from both sides: the same departure is folly to one man and an unrefusable duty of giving to another, and the woman left behind refuses to call either grace.

### siddhar:130: Siddhar 130 (பத்திரகிரியார், மெய்ஞ்ஞானப் புலம்பல்)
