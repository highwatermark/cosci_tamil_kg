## LINK akananuru:1 -> kural:1154 type=QUALIFIES
Note: Both speak in the woman's voice after a promise not to part is broken by his leaving: the Kural asks if the believer is at fault; Akananuru 1 asks whether he has forgotten (மறந்தனர் கொல்லோ).
Teaches: The Kural turns the broken promise into a question of blame, while the Sangam heroine keeps it a question of memory; the poem shows a woman who will not yet say he broke his word, which the couplet's moral question has already assumed.

### kural:1154: Kural 1154
Tamil: அளித்தஞ்சல் என்றவர் நீப்பின் தெளித்தசொல் / தேறியார்க்கு உண்டோ தவறு
