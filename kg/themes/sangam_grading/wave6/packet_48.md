Note: The couplet makes the parted woman's pallor spread the moment the man goes; the friends treat its absence as proof of deceit.
Teaches: The epic turns the Kural's sign of parting into a test applied by men to a wife, and shows the test failing, since a wife who knows her husband is safe does not pale.
