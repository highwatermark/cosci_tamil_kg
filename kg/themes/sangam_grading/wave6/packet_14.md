## LINK soolamani:34 -> kural:1228 type=TENSION
Note: Kural 1228 has the herdsman's flute as a weapon that kills the lonely woman at evening; verse 34 has the herdsmen's konrai flute so sweet that the asunam beast sleeps.
Teaches: The same herdsman's flute soothes the beast and wounds the parted woman, so its effect lies in the listener's state and not in the music, a point neither verse shows alone.
