## LINK thinaimozhi_aimpathu:24 -> kural:1154 type=QUALIFIES
Note: Thinaimozhi Aimpathu 24: the friend rests her comfort on the man who swore an oath (வஞ்சினம்) and held to it, with the rain as his token. Kural 1154: if he who said 'fear not' leaves, is there any fault in those who trusted his assuring word?
Teaches: The friend builds her comfort on his oath, while the couplet asks who is to blame if such an oath fails, so together they show that the whole mullai argument of waiting rests on trusting a man's word, and the Kural names the risk the friend leaves unsaid.
