Note: Pattinathar asks what forest life, eating wind, rags and the bowl avail without inner love, and says the Lord's true lovers reach release even living at home beside their women; Civakan says sin born of desire for women cannot be removed by union with women.
Teaches: The two agree that going to the forest is nothing in itself, but they part on the household: the Saiva poet lets love free one beside one's wife, while the Jain hero makes leaving the wife a condition, so the shared attack on outward renunciation hides opposite views of the married life.
