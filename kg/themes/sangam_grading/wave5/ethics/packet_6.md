## LINK pazhamozhi_nanooru:83 -> naladiyar:80 type=QUALIFIES
Note: Naladiyar 80 forbids eating from the hands of those one should not eat from and speaking words mixed with falsehood; Pazhamozhi 83 grants that the worthy may have eaten from another's hand and says they speak the truth even so.
Teaches: The Naladiyar guards truth by refusing the obligation that might bend it; Pazhamozhi allows the obligation and makes truth hold against it, a stronger and riskier claim.
List adds: it sets the worthy man's truth beside the traitor's borrowed lie in battle, a contrast of two men under obligation that the quatrain's separate refusals lack.
