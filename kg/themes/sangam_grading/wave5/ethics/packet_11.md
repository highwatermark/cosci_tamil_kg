Note: Kural 529 says kin who left return when the cause of discord is gone; Pazhamozhi 351 says kin who seem never to help will help at last, as rain falls after holding off.
Teaches: The couplet makes the return of kin wait on removing a cause, while the rain proverb makes it as sure as weather, so delay in kin's help is no proof of its absence.
