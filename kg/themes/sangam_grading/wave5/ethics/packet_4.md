Teaches: The same image works from two sides: the Naladiyar uses it for what the well-born still do in hardship, Pazhamozhi for how others must still regard the great when their standing is damaged.

## LINK pazhamozhi_nanooru:47 -> kural:894 type=SAME_IMAGE
Note: Kural 894 says the weak doing harm to the strong is beckoning Death; Pazhamozhi 47 says the strong tormenting the weak find the victims' tears fall on them as Death.
Teaches: The pair makes Death the outcome on both sides of an unequal fight: the weak who strike the strong invite it by their act, the strong who strike the weak receive it from their victims' tears.
List adds: it reverses the couplet's direction, making the strong the doer and the weak's tears, not the strong man's arm, the bearer of Death.
