## LINK pazhamozhi_nanooru:111 -> kural:972 type=TENSION
Note: Kural 972 says birth is alike for all and distinction comes from deeds; Pazhamozhi 111 lays the quarrelsome temper on those small by lineage. The conflict holds on the reading that குலத்துச் சிறியார் ranks by birth rather than naming the mean ones of a family.
Teaches: Set beside the Kural, the verse shows the ethics books divided on whether baseness is inherited: the couplet ranks by deeds, Pazhamozhi traces a vice to the family one comes from.
List adds: it attaches a specific behaviour, turning on peacemakers, to a birth group, the very link between birth and worth the couplet denies.

   2 சிலைத்தெழுந்து செம்மாப் பவரே - மலைத்தால்
   3 இழைத்த திகவா தவரைக் கனற்றிப்
   4 'பலிப்புறத் துண்பார் உணா'
