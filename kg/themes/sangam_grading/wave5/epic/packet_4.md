### naladiyar:99: Naladiyar 99
Tamil: இறப்பச் சிறிதென்னா தில்லென்னா தென்றும் / அறப்பயன் யார்மாட்டும் செய்க - முறைப்புதவின் / ஐயம் புகூஉம் தவசி கடிஞைபோல் / பைய நிறைத்து விடும்.
Pope: Say not ‘ 'tis passing little,' nor ‘ ' tis nought I give;' on all confer thy boon of virtuous charity, Like the dish the mendicant presents from door to door, by frequent doles 'twill be filled full.
Marked reading: Never say 'it is too little' or 'I have nothing'; always do the good of giving, to anyone, for like the ascetic's bowl filled at door after door, it fills slowly.
Marker's nuance: The bowl belongs to the one who receives, but it stands for what fills in the giver: Mudaliar says the fruit of virtue fills his life, Shanmuganar that good deeds accumulate.

## LINK neelakesi:512 -> siddhar:1729 type=TENSION
Note: Neelakesi says the Buddha's reasoning collapses into the void; the Siddhar tells his listener that all arising deeds are seen to be void, appearing without appearing in pure space.
Teaches: For the Jain debater reaching the void is a reductio, for the Siddhar it is the seeing that frees; the same word marks defeat in one tradition and insight in the other.
Gains: epic side: the Jain charge gains a witness that some Tamil teachers embraced the void as a goal, so the charge is aimed at a live position, not a straw one | other side: the Siddhar's void gains the objection it must meet, that a void reached by regress leaves nothing to know or hold
