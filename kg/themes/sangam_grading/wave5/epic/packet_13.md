Note: Naladiyar 20 urges keeping Death's stealth in mind and taking provisions in time; Thirikadugam names not reflecting on Death's certain coming as one of three things that lead to hell.
Teaches: The quatrain makes remembering death prudent; the verse makes forgetting it a sin with a penalty, so heedlessness is turned from folly into guilt.
