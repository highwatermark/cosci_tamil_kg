## LINK neelakesi:76 -> manimekalai:6:168-187 type=TENSION
Note: The Neelakesi's sage says the hells are seven (ஏழாய்) and spread into eighty-four hundred thousand dwellings; the Manimekalai's goddess summons the eight kinds of hell-beings (எண் வகை நரகர்) within the Chakravala.
Teaches: The two epics count the hells differently, seven earths stacked below the world against eight kinds of hell-dwellers within one world-system, so hell's shape is itself a point of sectarian difference.

## LINK manimekalai:26:33-41 -> silappathikaram:23:201-204 type=QUALIFIES
Note: The venba says gods worship the woman who worships her husband and that Kannaki has become the guest of the women of heaven; Kannaki here says she reached heaven by former good deeds and will leave it when that merit is spent.
Teaches: The Manimekalai accepts the Silappathikaram's heavenly welcome for Kannaki but makes it a stay that ends, so the earlier epic's final honour becomes one stage of a samsaric account whose real end is still to come.
