## LINK silappathikaram:7:194-197 -> kural:1207 type=QUALIFIES
Note: The couplet's woman asks what would become of her if she forgot, since she knows no forgetting and even remembering burns; Madhavi's woman says to the adumbu and the swans, let him go, we will not forget those who forgot us.
Teaches: In the Kural not forgetting is a helpless bind, since memory both burns and keeps her alive, whereas in the song it is announced as a choice with an audience, a reproach to the forgetter, so the pair shows the same refusal to forget used as a lament and as a charge.
Gains: epic side: the stanza gains the couplet's inner cost, that the vow not to forget is also a burning she cannot escape | other side: the couplet's private bind gains a public turn, the same memory declared to witnesses as an accusation against the one who forgot
