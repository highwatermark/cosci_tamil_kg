Note: Gotami, mourning her son at the burning ground, begged the goddess to take her life for his and was told a life gone goes by its deeds; the queen, mourning her son, is taught the same and her grief is quenched.
Teaches: The epic answers a mother's grief twice with the same doctrine but to opposite ends: Gotami, refused, dies beside her son, while the queen, taught to turn her love to all lives, is cleared and bows to her son's supposed enemy.
