Note: The Vanji ascetics foretell that the sea will take Kaveripattinam; in kaathai 25 the sea swallows it by the Manimekala goddess's curse and the ascetic and the mothers go to Vanji.

Note: In 127 the sage gives Neelakesi the nectar of dharma as the medicine for birth; in 722 she tells the Ajivika she was a demon until a great one gave her that medicine.
Teaches: The cure first given to the demoness becomes the debater's own credential: she argues not only from doctrine but from her own conversion, offering the opponent what she was given.
