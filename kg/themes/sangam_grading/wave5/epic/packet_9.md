Note: The Manimekalai's teacher says the first Jina (in Buddhist usage, the Buddha) taught only two means of knowledge, perception and inference; Neelakesi answers that on that rule there could be no medical treatise that cures disease.

Note: Tirumular says that as a madman is cleared by medicine, he was made clear when Nandi gave grace; Neelakesi says she, once a demon, was made clear by the dharma a teacher gave her.
