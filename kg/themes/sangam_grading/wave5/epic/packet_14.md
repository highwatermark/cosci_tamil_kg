## LINK manimekalai:25:200-221 -> siddhar:23 type=QUALIFIES
Note: Pathirakiriyar asks when he will see the bones within as white bones and himself lowered like a cast-off corpse; the king digs up his own former body, bones held together as if coated in white lime, and is bewildered.
Teaches: The epic grants literally the sight the Siddhar longs for, one's own body as white bones, and shows that the sight alone does not free: the king is bewildered until Manimekalai tells him what it is for.
Gains: epic side: a frame in which seeing one's own corpse is a discipline of release, against which the king's bewilderment is a failure to read it | other side: a test of its longing: the sight given in full, without the knowledge that would make it freeing
