# Which themes occur together in the Tamil?

We searched all 41,583 Tamil reading units available to this run in 45 corpus groups. A reading unit can be a couplet, a stanza, a section, or an entire hymn. The six sets of Tamil search terms concern composition, development, sensing, ecological dependence, cosmic scale, and pervasion or self. The examples below include passage identifiers and links to their Tamil editions.

## The count

| Distinct lenses matching a unit | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Units | 35,636 | 5,658 | 280 | 5 | 3 | 1 | 0 |

Only **289 units** matched two or more lenses. A zero means the specified strings were absent. Metaphor, synonyms, sandhi, or a different spelling can express the same idea. The results file gives all 63 possible nonempty sets, including combinations with zero matches. Inclusive counts allow other lenses to match, while exclusive counts require exactly the listed lenses. Each set includes a breakdown by corpus group. The results also list every multi-lens passage ID under its exact combination mask, giving readers a complete queue of 289 units. ## What changed under scrutiny?

* **Development and ecology** occurred together in 39 units across 14 corpus groups. The book- and length-adjusted expectation was 20.43. This was the only enrichment below the 0.05 familywise threshold (Holm-adjusted p = 0.0285). When we removed three short Tamil strings, the count fell to 8 against an expectation of 3.02 (adjusted p = 1). The result depends materially on how words are recognized. For example, in Patitruppattu 30 the development seed கரு also matches the beginning of the word for sugarcane. All 39 passages warrant word-level reading before inferring an association between the ideas. [Patitruppattu text](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0038.html)
* **Cosmic scale and pervasion or self** matched four Siddhar units, against 1.12 expected (adjusted p = 1). All four disappeared under the shorter vocabulary. In three, the single compound அண்டபிண்ட… can trigger both searches. The fourth, Kaduveli's Anandakkalippu 10, urges the hearer to discern the spiritual expanse and guide those who approach. In அண்டினோர்க், the opening letters matched a cosmic seed. Here the verb means *approach*. The line still matters to a reading of inward discipline. [Kaduveli 10](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0076.html) · [Puranamalai 37](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0276_02.html)
* **A real analogy can survive a poor match elsewhere in the same unit.** In Tiruvasagam 3, the opening five lines move from an immeasurable multitude to minute particles seen in a beam entering a house. This supports a poetic comparison across scales. The development lens also fires elsewhere in this long hymn on characters shared with compassion and thought. Particle imagery here supplies no measurement of particle size. [Tiruvasagam text, ID tiruvasagam:3](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0003_01.html)
* **The sole five-lens unit** spans 286 lines. Paddy occurs near the beginning, the five senses at line 81, and the cosmic-time word ஊழி at line 168. At line 233 the composition seed அணு occurs in அணுகுதலும், approaching a street. Reading the locations changes what the apparent five-way connection means. [Thirumurai text, ID thirumurai:11.12.7.1](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0127_02.html)

## How to reproduce and interpret the comparison

From the repository root, run `python3 -I scripts/science_combinations.py` with Python 3.10 or later. It regenerates combinations.json from the current Tamil corpus and vocabulary. The output records the input fingerprint, seed and group sizes so future additions can be distinguished from changes to the analysis. [Script](../../../scripts/science_combinations.py) · [Normalization and corpus loader](../../../scripts/science_inquiry.py)

1. Normalize Tamil to Unicode NFC and retain letters, combining marks and numbers. Search for substrings across each joined unit. This can find across word boundaries and creates false positives from inflected or unrelated words. Where parallel whole-poem and segmented files exist, use the segmented file. The 23 excess copies of identical normalized passages retain their original identifiers.
2. Enumerate 6 single lenses, 15 pairs, 20 triples, 15 groups of four, 6 groups of five and 1 group of six. Order adds no distinct co-occurrence. A combination can be evaluated even when no passage matches it.
3. Group units by corpus group and normalized length. Length bands are given by the integer part of twice the base-two logarithm of length. Within each of the 232 occupied groups, shuffle the units carrying each lens independently while retaining each lens's frequency. In 3,999 simulations per specification, count how often each intersection meets or exceeds its observed size. Add one to both numerator and denominator to obtain a one-sided Monte Carlo p-value.
4. Repeat after removing அணு, அண்ட and கரு, whose short forms match unrelated words or nested seeds. Use seeds 20261125 and 20261126 respectively. Apply Holm correction jointly across the 57 multi-lens hypotheses in each run (114 tests). The vocabulary already exists in the project and the sensitivity specification was chosen after inspecting ambiguous terms. These are exploratory checks. Neither run estimates translation accuracy, dating, or an effect in the physical world.

**Open work.** Read all 289 multi-lens units with the immediate Tamil phrase and the unit's speaker, genre and argument in view. Record whether terms share a clause, refer to the same thing, express an analogy, or merely occur far apart. Review single and zero matches for missed allusions using independently developed Tamil terms. Enrichment survived correction in the broad vocabulary only. No combination had corrected evidence across both runs.
