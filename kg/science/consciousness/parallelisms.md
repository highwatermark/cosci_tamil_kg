# From living bodies to the open universe

Six lenses retrieved 5,947 distinct units among 41,583 readable units across 45 corpus book IDs in this snapshot. A unit can be a couplet, sutra, hymn or passage of a longer poem. The search retrieved 180 composition candidates, 4,763 development candidates, 257 sensing candidates, 124 ecological dependence candidates, 885 cosmic scale candidates and 41 pervasion or self candidates. These overlap. The unusually broad development seed கரு can also occur in words for an idea, a dark colour, or a divine origin. These totals measure retrieval, not prevalence, accuracy or correlation.

Nine deliberately selected comparisons were read against the full local unit and its named scientific source. Seven are proposed and two physical explanations are withheld. The set illustrates the boundaries of six kinds of parallel. It is not a representative sample of the hits. The modern sources range from an anatomical atlas to field experiments, telescope data and collider observations. | Organizing question | Close reading | Modern counterpart and evidentiary limit |
|---|---|---|
| What are wholes made of, and why are their parts hard to see? | Manimekalai 27.108–157 reports an Ajivika teacher distinguishing life and four kinds of constituent. The account links their combination to mountains, trees and bodies. A hair invisible at dusk and a visible bundle explain how a collection can be perceptible. | The comparison concerns aggregation and resolving power. A modern experiment isolated and positioned xenon atoms on a surface. Attributing four elemental kinds, beginninglessness or indivisibility to modern atoms would erase substantive differences. [Eigler and Schweizer](https://research.ibm.com/publications/positioning-single-atoms-with-a-scanning-tunnelling-microscope) |
| How does a living form change over time? | Tiruvasagam 4 recounts dangers survived in the maternal womb, organized by months. Its repeated verb for surviving supplies the speaker’s purpose. | The developmental atlas orders embryos by anatomical features. Comparing what marks a transition is meaningful; equating a poetic month with a modern developmental stage needs independent anatomical evidence. [HDBR atlas](https://hdbratlas.org/carnegie_stages.html) |
| What distinguishes response, sensation and conscious experience? | Tolkappiyam Porul 9.27 distinguishes faculties and adds mind at the sixth. The next sutra places grasses and trees in the first class. The text offers a classification of living capacities. | Experiments can trace a signal from local plant damage to distant tissue. Signaling, discrimination, and subjective feeling require different evidence. The experiment directly addresses the first. [Toyota and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30213912/) |
| What keeps a body or community alive? | Purananuru 18 urges a ruler to bring water and land together, linking the management of water to food and the sustaining of life. | This is the strongest practical parallel. Water regime and yield can be measured separately. The poem also asks who is responsible for provision; a plant physiology result cannot answer that political question. [Rice water-regime experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3539349/) |
| How can one reason about immense scales or durations? | Tiruvasagam 3 compares worlds with motes in a beam entering a house. Paripadal 2 places the succession of cosmic conditions across ages. These are different uses of scale: an optical image for multiplicity and a narrative ordering of time. | Modern cosmology infers parameters from observations under explicit assumptions. A poem can encode an ordering or a change of viewpoint without supplying a distance, an expansion rate or a date. [Planck Collaboration](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/13afa496-7458-438a-b3a1-1354471b2bff) |
| What would it mean for one reality to be present throughout many things? | Paripadal 3 moves from heat in fire and fragrance in a flower to truth in speech and love in virtue, addressing the same “you.” Pattinathar’s Puranamalai 37 moves between the minute, body and cosmos while acknowledging the speaker’s limits. | Philosophical comparison can ask whether the relation is constitution, dependence, presence or identity. In quantum field theory a particular field enters a quantitative model with interaction and measurement predictions. ATLAS supplies an example. Neither passage selects such a field or establishes a link between field dynamics and consciousness. [ATLAS experimental report](https://cds.cern.ch/record/1511050) |

## Tests of the reading

* **Preserve the speaker.** The atomist passage is attributed dialogue inside an epic. Preserve the teacher’s attribution when connecting it to commentary, another school’s criticism, or particle physics.
* **Vary the local context.** Manimekalai 20.104–119 repeats அணுகல் as a prohibition on approaching. Compare it with chapter 27 before permitting an atomism label. In Kuruntokai 3 earth, sky and water measure the scale of affection. The object of comparison is a relationship. In Tiruvasagam 228 a divine address carries கருவாய்; the morphology of an embryo is a poor match.
* **Specify the observable.** Classify a candidate by what the text gives: an intervention, observable sequence, explanation, analogy, theological claim or unresolved reading. For an intervention ask what changed and how it was observed. For a scientific mechanism ask what outcome could count against it.
* **Evaluate discovery separately from prevalence.** For the next evaluation sample positives, false friends and no-keyword units across works and genres. Blind the two Tamil readers to the proposed modern counterpart first. Then ask them to name the exact shared relation and the first place the analogy fails. Compare context-aware retrieval with literal search on whole held-out works, measuring precision, recovery of independently located witnesses and unsupported mechanism claims. Corpus-wide correlation would require independent annotation and controls for length, genre, duplication and dating.

## Reproduce

Run `python3 -I scripts/science_inquiry.py scan` for the current snapshot and `python3 -I scripts/science_inquiry.py check` to verify the graph endpoints and literal Tamil anchors. Add `--book manimekalai` to the scan to restrict counts to one book. Use `find 'பரமாணு' 'அணுத் தெய்வக் கண்ணோர்'` with the same script for passage text. The scanner normalizes printed whitespace and punctuation but does not perform morphological analysis.
