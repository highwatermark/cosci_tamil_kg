# One reality, the disappearing I, and the meaning of energy

## Read the adjacent verses first

In Pattinathar’s *Puranamalai* 59 the addressee is பூரணமே, “O Fullness.” **சத்திசிவமிரண்டாய்த்தான்முடிவிலொன்றாகிச்** describes Shakti and Shiva as two and ultimately one. Verse 60 changes the inventory: sensory faculties, the senses or their objects, distinctions among the elements, and **அறிவாகி**, “as knowing.” Its ending **அளவறியேன்** says the speaker cannot comprehend the extent. Read together, the pair offers a more specific thesis than an isolated “everything is one”: the divine addressed by the poet is present on both sides of knowing, in the differentiated world and in the capacity to apprehend it. This last sentence is an inference across two verses. The grammar leaves a speaker addressing a “you.” Translating Shakti as divine power is reasonable in context; assigning that power physical units adds a new claim. ## Three modern questions with different tests

1. **Could the cosmos be a single subject?** Cosmopsychism gives this proposition a precise form. Itay Shani asks how individual experiencing subjects can arise from a consciousness fundamental to the whole. Pattinathar offers a comparative text about the relation of knower and known. A useful disagreement is how to account for genuinely different perspectives if their ground is one. [Shani](https://doi.org/10.1080/05568641.2015.1106709)
2. **Can someone experience a weakened self-boundary?** A meditation study used MEG to compare instructed self-boundary states. Within-person classification was more successful than generalization across people. This motivates separating a change in bodily boundaries from surrender, devotion and the claim of cosmic identity in a reader’s coding. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12742028/)
3. **Does a surrounding field supply energy or information?** A 2026 replication measured an alternating magnetic field and participants’ reports of flicker, supporting a retinal response. McFadden’s separate hypothesis concerns the brain’s own electromagnetic field. Neither source measured energy gained through Siddhar practice or transmission from a conscious universe. For a physical claim establish the proposed field, coupling, receiver and quantitative observable. For net energy specify the system boundary and count energy inputs and outputs. For information specify an independently generated target and a blinded, chance-calibrated prediction. A claim of cosmic subjectivity would still need a further argument even after a coupling were demonstrated. [Field experiment](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-44440-5) · [Brain-field hypothesis](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niaa016)
